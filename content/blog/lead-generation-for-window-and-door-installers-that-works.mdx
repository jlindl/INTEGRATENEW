A homeowner decides to replace their windows or fit a new composite door, and the first thing they usually do is search online and fill in two or three enquiry forms within the same afternoon. Lead generation for window and door installers is really a race to be the business that responds first, with a quote that fits what the homeowner actually needs. Win that race consistently and your diary fills with real jobs. Lose it, and you end up bidding against two or three other installers for the same conservatory or bifold enquiry, often days after the homeowner has already picked someone else.

Most window and door installers still rely heavily on referrals, a static website and general enquiry forms from comparison sites. That works reasonably well when things are steady, but it leaves you exposed the moment referrals slow down, and it hands control of your enquiries to third-party portals that often sell the same lead to your competitors. Businesses that keep a full diary of double glazing, composite door, bifold and conservatory work tend to have built something more deliberate: a way of finding the right homeowners, in the right postcodes, and responding to them faster than anyone else can.

## The problem with relying on referrals and quote portals

Referrals are the best leads any installer gets, but they are not something you can control or scale. They dry up when your fitters are booked solid for a few months and word of mouth quietly stops.

Quote comparison sites fill that gap, but they bring problems of their own. The same enquiry is often sold to three or four installers at once, so you are racing on price against people who have not even seen the property. Targeting is loose too: you get people wanting to price up a single repair alongside genuine buyers ready to commit to a full house of bifolds, all mixed into the same batch of leads.

Speed is the other issue. A homeowner who submits an enquiry for a broken composite door lock at eight in the evening wants a reply that evening, not a callback the next afternoon once your office reopens. If a competitor answers first, the job is often gone before you have even seen the message.

## Lead generation for window and door installers: how it actually works

The alternative is to build a system that finds the right people and responds to them before anyone else can, without you having to sit by your phone all evening. In practice this runs in four stages.

1. **Targeted Meta ads.** Facebook and Instagram ads are shown to homeowners within a set radius of your working area, using imagery and copy built around the actual job types you want more of, whether that is composite doors, bifold sets, conservatory replacements or repairs.
2. **An instant lead form.** Instead of sending people to a long website form, the ad opens a short form inside Facebook or Instagram itself, so there is almost no friction between seeing the ad and getting in touch.
3. **An AI WhatsApp agent replies immediately.** Within moments, the enquiry gets a message asking the questions that matter: what the job is, roughly what budget they have in mind, whether it is their own property, and how soon they want it done.
4. **Qualified jobs land in your calendar.** Once the agent has established that the enquiry is genuine and matches the work you do, it books a survey or a call straight into your diary, at a time that suits you.

The value is in how the system treats different enquiries differently. A storm-damaged conservatory roof and someone browsing bifold prices for a summer extension are not the same lead, and they should not be handled the same way. The agent can flag the repair as urgent and push for a quick booking, while parking the bifold enquiry for a call once the homeowner has firmed up their plans. A similar approach for another trade is covered in [an AI WhatsApp assistant for joiners that qualifies every lead](/blog/ai-whatsapp-assistant-for-joiners), and the same logic applies just as well to window and door work.

## What good lead generation looks like for an installer

When this is set up well, a few things become obvious quite quickly.

- Enquiries get a reply within a minute or two, at any time of day, not just office hours.
- The mix of jobs coming through matches what you actually want to fit: if your business does not touch conservatories, your ads and questions should filter those out early.
- Your diary fills with booked surveys rather than a pile of unread messages that someone has to work through manually.
- Budget and timing are known before you drive out, so you are not quoting a premium aluminium bifold to someone who was expecting a basic uPVC repair.

## Common mistakes window and door installers make

- **Targeting too wide an area.** A radius that covers an hour's drive in every direction spreads the ad spend thin and pulls in people outside your realistic working patch.
- **Running one generic ad for everything.** A homeowner looking at composite doors and one looking at full conservatory replacements respond to different images and different messages. Treating them as the same audience wastes budget.
- **Skipping qualification entirely.** Sending every raw enquiry straight to a salesperson means hours spent on people who were only ever window shopping.
- **Forgetting the website behind the ad.** Even a homeowner who messages instantly will often check your site and reviews before agreeing to a survey. If that site looks dated or slow, some of that trust disappears. [Web design](/web-design) built for trades matters more here than most installers expect.
- **Judging results after a few days.** Window and door jobs are considered purchases. Some enquiries convert within hours, others take weeks of thinking before a homeowner books a survey.

## How to judge whether it is working

The number that matters most is not cost per lead, it is cost per booked survey, and further along, cost per signed job. A cheap lead that never picks up the phone is worth less than a more expensive one that turns into a bifold order.

A few things worth tracking:

- How many enquiries turn into a booked survey appointment.
- How many of those surveys actually show up, rather than being no-shows.
- How many surveys convert into signed work, and roughly how that splits across repairs, single door jobs and full house installs.
- How results shift with the seasons: repair enquiries tend to spike after bad weather, while conservatory and bifold enquiries build through spring as people plan summer extensions.

Give any new system a proper run, at least four to six weeks, before judging it. Early weeks are for tightening the targeting and the questions the AI agent asks. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much does lead generation cost for a window and door installer?

It depends heavily on your area, how much competition there is for the same postcodes, and which products you are advertising. Bifolds and full conservatory replacements tend to attract a higher cost per lead than a simple repair enquiry, because the audience is smaller and the job value is higher. Rather than fixating on cost per lead, it is more useful to work out what you can afford to pay per booked survey once you know your close rate.

### Do Meta ads actually work for high-ticket jobs like bifolds and conservatories?

They can, provided the targeting and the follow-up are built around the fact that this is a considered purchase, not an impulse buy. The ad does not need to close the sale on its own. Its job is to get the right homeowner into a conversation quickly, so the qualification stage and the survey visit can do the rest.

### What happens to leads that are not ready to buy yet?

A good AI WhatsApp agent asks about timing as part of qualification, so someone who is just starting to plan a conservatory for next spring is not treated the same as someone whose composite door has stopped locking. Those slower enquiries can be flagged for a follow-up call further down the line instead of being chased immediately or dropped altogether.

### Will this replace referrals and word of mouth?

No, and it is not meant to. Referrals will always be some of your best work, because the trust is already there. This kind of system exists to give you a second, more predictable source of enquiries alongside referrals, so a quiet month for word of mouth does not mean a quiet month for the business.

### How is this different from buying leads through a quote comparison site?

The main difference is exclusivity and speed. Ads run for your business only, sent to your audience, and the reply comes from you within minutes rather than from a lead that has already been sold to several installers at once and sat in an inbox for hours.
